menu_book Preparation Steps

  1. 1

    Grind 18g of House Blend beans finely. Tamp evenly into the portafilter.

  2. 2

    Extract a double shot of espresso (approx 36g) into the serving cup.

  3. 3

    Steam 180ml of fresh milk until microfoam forms and temperature reaches 60-65°C.

  4. 4

    Pour steamed milk over the espresso, finishing with a classic tulip latte art.
